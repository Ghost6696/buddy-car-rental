Booking & Caren.io

How does the Caren.io integration work? expand_more
Our platform is fully integrated with Caren.io to provide real-time availability and instant booking confirmation. When you select a vehicle, the reservation is immediately locked in the Caren.io global distribution system, ensuring your car is ready when you arrive.
Can I modify my booking after confirmation? expand_more
Yes, you can modify your booking up to 24 hours before pickup through the 'Manage Booking' link in your confirmation email. Modifications are subject to vehicle availability and potential price adjustments based on new dates or locations.

Insurance & Protection

What insurance coverage is included? expand_more
All rentals include standard Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) and Third Party Liability. We offer premium "Zero-Deductible" packages during the checkout process for total peace of mind, covering everything from glass to tires.
Is there a security deposit required? expand_more
Yes, a temporary authorization hold is placed on your credit card at the time of pickup. The amount varies depending on the vehicle category and your chosen insurance package. This hold is typically released within 3-5 business days after returning the vehicle.

Fleet & Requirements

What is the minimum age to rent? expand_more
The minimum age is 21 for standard vehicles and 25 for our premium/exotic fleet. Drivers between 21-24 may be subject to a "Young Driver" daily fee depending on the location and vehicle class.
Are the cars in the photos the exact ones? expand_more
For our Premium and Exotic categories, we guarantee the specific make and model. For our Standard fleet, we guarantee the vehicle category and features, though the exact color or year might vary slightly based on local availability.
